Margins Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Margins is an EPUB reader. It's built so that your books and your reading stay yours.

What we collect

Nothing. Margins has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data about you, your books or how you read.

What stays on your device

The books you import, your highlights, notes, tags, bookmarks, reading sessions, goals and statistics are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. The home-screen widget reads a small summary from that same storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Readwise (optional)

If you choose to connect Readwise, Margins sends the highlights you export (quote, note, tags, book title and author, and location) directly from your device to Readwise, using the access token you enter. The token is kept in the iOS Keychain. Nothing is sent unless you turn this on, and you can disconnect at any time in Settings. Readwise's handling of that data is covered by Readwise's privacy policy.

Exports and sharing

When you export highlights or share a quote image, the file goes only where you send it through the iOS share sheet.

Children

Margins collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
